How the numbers are worked out
  • Each item gets its cheapest source: crafted, ascended from the level below, dropped by a quest, or found on a map run. Ingredients are priced the same way, all the way down.
  • Maps cost orbs to make and drop orbs, so the page solves both together until the prices settle (a few seconds).
  • Prices are the game's base sell values, before haggling, customer preferences and shop bonuses.
  • Not counted: hero time, events and the weekly shop, modded ingredients, stronger enemies on enchanted maps.

Model output from the game's own data tables, not observed in play. Fan project, not affiliated with the developers of Merchant.
