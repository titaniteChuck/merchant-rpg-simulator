Merchant RPG Simulator

Unofficial balancing tools for Merchant: crafting profit, essence sources, save viewer and battle simulation.

Tools

  • 📜

    Save

    Open your save to see your heroes with their real combat stats, their gear, your inventory value, maps and crafters. Nothing is uploaded.

    Available
  • ⚔️

    Battle

    Put your heroes in a party and simulate hundreds of quest fights or map runs with the game's combat rules.

    Available
  • 📖

    Wiki

    Bare stats of everything: skills, passives, equipment, consumables, materials, maps and hero classes, all cross-linked.

    Available
  • 🪙

    Profit calculator

    Rank items by sell value against full production cost: quests, maps, crafting, ascension and enchanting.

    Available
  • 🌾

    Farming planner

    Where should your heroes spend their time? Quests and map runs simulated with your party, ranked by gold and essence per hour.

    Available
  • 📈

    Levelling

    Hours to max level on the best XP quest at every level, and what the next ascension gives against gold and the hours to level back up.

    Available

Fan project, not affiliated with the developers of Merchant. No game files are hosted here.
