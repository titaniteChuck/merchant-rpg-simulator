Save viewer

Open your save to see your heroes with their real combat stats, their gear, your inventory value, maps and crafters.

Where is my save file?
  • Windows: %APPDATA%\Retora\Merchant\Documents (paste it in the file dialog's address bar).
  • Linux / Steam Deck (Proton): <Steam library>/steamapps/compatdata/346750/pfx/drive_c/users/steamuser/AppData/Roaming/Retora/Merchant/Documents
  • Slot 1 is PlayerData.txt, slots 2 to 5 are Slot2Data.txt … Slot5Data.txt. Backups (…Backup1.txt) work too.
